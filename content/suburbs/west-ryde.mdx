localNotes:
  - heading: Side setbacks are the usual reason a rear extension needs a DA
    body: >-
      Narrower lots mean many West Ryde houses already sit at or inside the
      minimum side setback that complying development requires. If the
      existing house is non-compliant, extending along the same wall line
      usually pushes you into a development application with the City of Ryde.
      This is worth checking before you commission a design, not after.
  - heading: Reconfiguring inside the existing footprint is often the better buy
    body: >-
      Because the lots are tighter, the return on opening up the inside of a
      West Ryde house is unusually good compared to extending it. A kitchen and
      living reconfiguration costs a third of what a rear extension costs, does
      not touch the setbacks, frequently needs no planning approval at all, and
      on a 1960s floor plan it often delivers most of what the family actually
      wanted.
  - heading: Overland flow near the creek lines
    body: >-
      Parts of the suburb sit on overland flow paths. If your property is
      affected, the flood certificate sets your minimum floor levels before
      design starts, and it can rule out a slab at the level you were
      expecting. It is a ten dollar certificate and a five minute check.
faqs:
  - question: Do I need approval to remove a wall between my kitchen and living room?
    answer: >-
      You do not need planning approval for internal work that does not change
      the building footprint, but you do need a structural engineer's design
      if the wall is load bearing, and the work has to comply with the
      Building Code. If the house was built before 1990 you also need the
      asbestos position established before demolition starts. We handle all
      three.
  - question: How much does it cost to open up a kitchen and living area in West Ryde?
    answer: >-
      Our kitchen and living reconfigurations have fallen between $90,000 and
      $180,000 including GST. The single biggest variable is what the wall is
      carrying, and the second is whether the new beam can be hidden in the
      ceiling or has to sit below it in a bulkhead.
  - question: How do I know whether my house has asbestos?
    answer: >-
      If it was built or altered before 1990, assume it might until a sample
      says otherwise. We arrange sampling during the pre-price investigation
      so that licensed removal is a known, quoted line item in your fixed
      price rather than a variation after demolition starts.
  - question: Which council covers West Ryde?
    answer: >-
      City of Ryde. Ryde applies its own development control plan on top of
      the state codes, and it is one of the stricter councils in the area on
      side setbacks for additions.
