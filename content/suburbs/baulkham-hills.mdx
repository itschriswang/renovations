localNotes:
  - heading: The 1990s rear addition is the thing that decides your second storey
    body: >-
      An enormous number of houses here had a family room added at the back
      twenty to thirty years ago. Those additions frequently sit on a slab
      that is neither tied into the original nor edge-thickened, and it will
      not carry a second storey without underpinning and a new edge beam. That
      is a five figure problem, and finding it before you sign a contract is
      the difference between a design that works and a variation that hurts.
      We will not price a second storey without an engineer physically
      inspecting the footings.
  - heading: Most lots here qualify for complying development
    body: >-
      The lot sizes and setbacks in this suburb mean a large share of second
      storey additions can be approved by a private certifier under the Codes
      SEPP rather than through a development application to The Hills Shire.
      The usual limits are the height plane, the building envelope and
      overshadowing of the neighbour's living room windows, all of which are
      tested at concept stage.
  - heading: Trees, not bulk and scale
    body: >-
      The complication here is more often a tree than a planning control. The
      Hills Shire protects a wide range of species and sizes, and a mature tree
      inside your build area will need consent before it is touched. Factor
      the assessment into your programme rather than discovering it at
      excavation.
faqs:
  - question: Can my Baulkham Hills house take a second storey?
    answer: >-
      Most can, but not all, and the ones that cannot usually look identical
      from the street to the ones that can. It depends on the existing
      footings and slab, and particularly on whether any part of the house was
      added later on a separate slab. We excavate an inspection pit and have a
      structural engineer look at the footings before we give you a price.
  - question: How much does a second storey addition cost here?
    answer: >-
      Our second storey additions have fallen between $380,000 and $700,000
      including GST. Add the cost of renting somewhere for the period the house
      is uninhabitable, which is usually three to four months and which we put
      on the programme at contract stage so you can plan for it.
  - question: Do I need a DA for a second storey in The Hills Shire?
    answer: >-
      Frequently not. If your lot and your design meet the Codes SEPP
      standards, a private certifier can issue a complying development
      certificate in around three to five weeks. A development application to
      The Hills Shire would typically add ten to sixteen weeks.
  - question: Can we live in the house while the second storey is built?
    answer: >-
      For part of it. On most jobs the family stays for the early weeks and
      moves out for the period when the roof is off and the stair is cut in.
      We put those dates on the construction programme before you sign, so the
      rental can be booked properly.
