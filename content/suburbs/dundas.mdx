localNotes:
  - heading: Assume asbestos until a sample says otherwise
    body: >-
      In fibro and part fibro houses of this era, asbestos cement sheeting is
      the default assumption. Licensed removal is not a disaster and it is not
      expensive relative to the job — what makes it expensive is finding it
      after demolition has started. We sample during the pre-price
      investigation so removal is a known line item in a fixed price rather
      than a variation.
  - heading: The valley is steeper than it looks from the main roads
    body: >-
      Cross falls of a metre or more across a building envelope are common
      through Dundas Valley. That brings in retaining, drainage and often a
      split level or a suspended floor. It is the main reason two apparently
      identical houses in this suburb can be $60,000 apart on the same scope.
  - heading: Replacing fibro cladding is often the right move
    body: >-
      If the sheeting is coming off in one area anyway, doing the whole
      envelope at once while the scaffold and the licensed removalist are
      already on site costs materially less than doing it in stages later, and
      it resolves the insulation and weatherproofing at the same time.
faqs:
  - question: My house is fibro. Can it still be renovated?
    answer: >-
      Yes, and thoroughly. Fibro houses renovate well and often have a simple,
      workable structure underneath. The difference is that the demolition
      stage is a licensed asbestos removal with a control plan and a clearance
      certificate, and that has to be planned and quoted rather than improvised.
  - question: How much does asbestos removal add to a renovation?
    answer: >-
      It depends entirely on how much there is and where. Once we have sample
      results it is a firm, quotable number rather than an allowance, which is
      exactly why we test before we price.
  - question: Can I remove the asbestos myself to save money?
    answer: >-
      No, and please do not. In New South Wales there are strict limits on
      unlicensed asbestos work, the disposal requirements are specific, and the
      health risk is real and permanent. Licensed removal on a job of this size
      is a small share of the total.
  - question: Which council covers Dundas?
    answer: >-
      City of Parramatta, covering Dundas, Dundas Valley and Telopea.
