localNotes:
  - heading: The amalgamation still affects which controls apply
    body: >-
      Epping moved into the City of Parramatta in 2016, and the development
      control plan that applies to a given block may still be the legacy one
      from the former council area. Do not assume the current Parramatta DCP
      is the operative document for your street without checking.
  - heading: Conservation areas change what you can do to the front
    body: >-
      In a heritage conservation area, work visible from the street is
      controlled in a way it is not elsewhere, and complying development is
      generally not available. That does not stop you renovating. It usually
      pushes the work to the rear and means a development application rather
      than a certificate, which is a timeline difference more than a design
      one.
  - heading: Bushfire prone land on the northern edges
    body: >-
      Parts of the suburb bordering the bushland reserves are mapped as
      bushfire prone. If yours is, a bushfire attack level assessment comes
      first and it affects glazing, cladding, decking and screening. It is a
      cost you need in the budget from day one, not one you discover at
      documentation.
faqs:
  - question: Is my Epping house in a heritage conservation area?
    answer: >-
      A number of Epping streets are, and it is not obvious from looking at
      them. We check the planning certificate for your property at feasibility
      stage, which is where any heritage listing or conservation area will be
      recorded.
  - question: Can I still extend a house in a conservation area?
    answer: >-
      Almost always, yes. The controls are principally about what is visible
      from the street and about retaining the character of the streetscape.
      Rear extensions are usually achievable. You will generally need a
      development application rather than a complying development certificate.
  - question: Which council covers Epping?
    answer: >-
      City of Parramatta, since the 2016 amalgamations. Epping was previously
      in Hornsby Shire, and some legacy planning controls still apply.
  - question: How long does approval take in Epping?
    answer: >-
      If complying development is available, three to six weeks. If you need a
      development application to the City of Parramatta, allow twelve to twenty
      weeks, and longer again if heritage referral is involved.
