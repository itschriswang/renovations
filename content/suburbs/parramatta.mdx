localNotes:
  - heading: Heritage conservation areas are extensive
    body: >-
      Parramatta has substantial heritage conservation areas and a large number
      of individually listed items. If your property is affected, complying
      development is generally not available and the work visible from the
      street is controlled. Rear extensions remain achievable, and the
      constraint is usually about timeline and documentation rather than about
      whether you can renovate at all.
  - heading: Flooding near the river and the creek lines
    body: >-
      Land near the Parramatta River and the tributary creeks is flood
      affected, and the flood planning level sets your minimum habitable floor
      level. On an affected lot this determines the design before anything is
      drawn, and it can make a ground floor extension considerably more
      involved than it first appears.
  - heading: Zoning has changed on many streets
    body: >-
      Planning controls along the corridors have changed repeatedly in recent
      years. This rarely stops a renovation, but it can affect what is
      permissible and it is worth understanding what your land is now zoned for
      before you spend on a design.
faqs:
  - question: Is my Parramatta property heritage listed?
    answer: >-
      It is recorded on the planning certificate for the property, which shows
      both individual heritage items and conservation areas. We obtain it at
      feasibility stage before design work begins.
  - question: Can I renovate a heritage listed house?
    answer: >-
      Yes. Heritage controls are principally about protecting significant
      fabric and the streetscape, not about preventing change. It generally
      means a development application with a heritage impact statement rather
      than a complying development certificate, and it means the rear of the
      house carries more of the work.
  - question: What does a flood affected property mean for my extension?
    answer: >-
      It sets a minimum floor level for habitable rooms and can affect
      materials and structure below that level. It is established from the
      council's flood information before design, because it is not something
      that can be adjusted afterwards.
  - question: Which council covers Parramatta?
    answer: >-
      City of Parramatta, which since 2016 also covers Epping, Carlingford,
      Dundas and parts of several neighbouring suburbs.
