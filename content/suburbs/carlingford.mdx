localNotes:
  - heading: Sewer mains cross a lot of rear yards here
    body: >-
      The subdivision pattern put Sydney Water mains through the back of many
      Carlingford blocks rather than along the street. If you are extending to
      the rear, find out where the main runs before you fall in love with a
      plan. It does not necessarily stop you building — a build-over
      application with a pier and beam floor is a normal and approvable answer
      — but it changes the structure, the cost and the programme, and it is
      much cheaper to know at concept stage than at excavation.
  - heading: The lots are wide enough that complying development usually works
    body: >-
      Most Carlingford lots comfortably meet the minimum width and setback
      requirements for complying development, which means a certifier can
      approve a single storey rear extension or a second storey in weeks
      rather than months. The exceptions are the battle-axe blocks created by
      later subdivision, where the access handle affects how the lot is
      measured.
  - heading: Slope toward the creek lines
    body: >-
      The land falls away toward the watercourses on the eastern and southern
      edges of the suburb. On those streets a rear extension often needs cut
      and fill, retaining and drainage, none of which you can see in the
      finished job and all of which you pay for. A level check at the first
      site visit tells us within about ten minutes whether this applies to you.
faqs:
  - question: Do I need council approval to extend my Carlingford house?
    answer: >-
      Often not. If your lot meets the size and setback requirements in the
      Codes SEPP and your design stays inside the standards, a private
      certifier can issue a complying development certificate, typically in
      three to six weeks. If any part of the design falls outside those
      standards you need a development application to the City of Parramatta,
      which is currently taking substantially longer. We work out which
      pathway applies to your block during feasibility, before any design work
      that depends on the answer.
  - question: How much does a rear extension cost in Carlingford?
    answer: >-
      Our ground floor rear extensions have fallen between $220,000 and
      $420,000 including GST. On a Carlingford block the two things most
      likely to push you toward the upper half of that band are a sewer main
      through the build area and a cross fall that brings in retaining.
  - question: How long will it take from first call to moving back in?
    answer: >-
      For a rear extension approved as complying development, expect around 40
      to 50 weeks in total. Roughly half of that is design, documentation and
      approval before anyone picks up a tool.
  - question: Which council covers Carlingford?
    answer: >-
      City of Parramatta. Carlingford moved into the Parramatta local
      government area in the 2016 amalgamations, and the planning controls
      that apply to your block may still depend on which former council area
      your street sat in. We check this on every job rather than assuming.
