  - heading: Estate covenants can still apply
    body: >-
      Some of the newer estates carry restrictive covenants registered on
      title that control external materials, roof form and fencing
      independently of the council's planning controls. A covenant is a
      private legal restriction, not a planning one, and complying development
      approval does not override it. We check the title, not just the planning
      certificate.

      walls that appear to be partitions.

    answer: >-

      commonly used in newer estates to control external appearance. It is
      found on the title search, not on the planning certificate, and it
      applies regardless of what the council or a certifier approves.
